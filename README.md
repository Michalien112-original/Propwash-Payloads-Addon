# Propwash: Payloads

Strap a charge under your FPV drone, dive, and make it count.

[CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/propwash-payloads) | [Official Trailer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FWM5HiqfCkU)

## About

Payloads is an add-on for [Propwash FPV](https://github.com/LardTom/propwash-fpv) that turns your quads into strike drones, and gives everyone on the ground ways to fight back: AA turrets, signal jammers and (soon with TaCZ) actual guns.

Requires Propwash FPV 0.5.0 · Minecraft 26.3 · Fabric · Java 25. Install on the server and every client.

## Charges

17 charges, from a 45 g mini frag for your whoop up to a 3 kg mega warhead that needs an X-class lifter. Mount one in the bottom slot of your drone (where the LED strip goes) in the Drone Workbench. Your action cam still fits on top, and every charge is paintable.

## Dropping mode

Press G (rebindable under Controls) while flying armed and the charge comes off. It keeps your drone's speed, falls and goes off on whatever it hits, including water. Your drone gets lighter right away and flies on.

## AA Turrets

Two turrets that lock onto armed drones and shoot at them. They don't destroy drones outright: every hit damages one part (the battery, a motor or a prop), and the drone keeps flying worse and worse until it goes down. The pilot sees a LOCK warning in the goggles, with which way the turret is and how far, and hears a beep that speeds up as the lock closes.

## Signal Jammer

A box with four antennas. Fly near one and your video fills with static and your link quality drops. Inside about 40% of its 48-block radius, your control link is cut and Propwash's failsafe takes over. A redstone signal turns it off.

## FPV Monitor

Place it, right-click it, and watch the goggle feed of whoever placed it. Sneak + right-click to tune it to yourself. Only the people who switched a monitor on receive the feed, and the pilot only sends frames while someone is watching. Perfect for spectators, or for a co-pilot spotting targets.
